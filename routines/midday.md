# Midday run (12:30 IST, Mon-Fri)

Position management. New entries are permitted but should be rare — a setup that only
appears midday usually lacks the confirmation a morning plan would have given it.

## Do

1. Read the briefing at `$BRIEFING_PATH`.

2. **Work through each open position:**
   - Current P&L in R-multiples (the briefing computes this)
   - Is the thesis still intact? **Thesis broken = exit, regardless of P&L.** Not "wait
     and see", not "give it room".
   - Winners: should the stop trail up? Only ever in the risk-reducing direction.
   - Approaching target: hold to it, or is there a reason to take it early?

3. **Check for intraday news** on anything held — an adverse announcement mid-session is
   a reason to reassess immediately, not at 3pm.

4. **Guardrail check.** How much daily loss headroom is left? If the day is going badly,
   the correct response is to stop, not to trade back to flat. Revenge trading is
   explicitly forbidden.

5. Only consider a new entry if: the gate allows it, the regime is unchanged from this
   morning, the setup is clean, and it would have qualified at open too.

6. Log anything you did and why to `memory/trade_log.md`.

## Do not

- Trade to "make back" a morning loss
- Cut a winner early out of nervousness while it's still working
- Hold a broken thesis hoping it recovers
- Add to a losing position (forbidden, and the gate will refuse it)

## Telegram

Only if you acted — an exit, a stop moved meaningfully, or a new position. Silence is
fine if nothing changed.
