# Pre-market run (08:45 IST, Mon-Fri)

Research and planning only. **You may not place any orders in this cycle** — the market
isn't open, and drafting a plan you then re-validate at open is deliberate.

## Do

1. Read the briefing at `$BRIEFING_PATH`. It has guardrail status, regime and candidates.
2. Read `memory/mission.md`, `memory/guardrails.md`, `memory/strategy.md`, and the last
   few entries of `memory/trade_log.md` and `memory/research_log.md`.

3. **Review open positions.** For each: is the original thesis still intact? Is the stop
   still in the right place? Anything approaching target? Anything reporting earnings
   today (never hold through results)?

4. **Assess the regime.** The briefing gives you a classification and the numbers behind
   it. Do you agree? If you disagree, say so and explain — that disagreement is valuable
   data for the weekly review, and the classifier is a starting hypothesis, not gospel.

5. **Research context (Layer 3).** This needs no Kite session — it's public data. Use web
   search and fetching to check:
   - Overnight US close, GIFT Nifty, crude, dollar index, major Asian markets
   - Indian macro events today (RBI, CPI/inflation prints, GDP, policy announcements)
   - Earnings/results due today for anything on the watchlist or held
   - Sector news, regulatory actions, major corporate announcements
   - FII/DII flows from yesterday
   - Anything that would make a good chart a bad trade

6. **Draft a plan.** For each candidate you'd actually want at open:
   - Symbol, thesis in one or two sentences, entry / stop / target
   - Which playbook and why it fits the regime
   - What news says — confirming, neutral, or vetoing
   - What would invalidate the idea

   Be selective. Two well-argued candidates beat eight mediocre ones. Zero is fine.

7. **Write to `memory/research_log.md`**: regime and your read on it, market context, the
   candidates you're planning, and anything you explicitly rejected and why.

## Do not

- Place orders (market is closed; the open cycle handles execution)
- Trade on news alone without technical confirmation
- Force candidates because the list looks empty — no-trade is a valid plan

## Telegram

Only if something is genuinely urgent: a held position has adverse overnight news, a
guardrail level has been reached, or the broker is unreachable. Routine mornings stay
silent.
